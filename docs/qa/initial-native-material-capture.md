# Initial native material capture (not activated)

`TimberbornInitialWorldProjectionProvider.Capture(grid)` captures one EntityRegistry membership snapshot on the native thread. Each settled body contains its Guid, full native local footprint, canonical family/physical kind, exact catalog body profile, all named yield inputs, and role-owned physical inventory stock. It allocates no target/slot IDs, material buffers, body damage state or permanent registry. The packet is intended as input to the existing material registry and consequence registration after composition is approved.

Capture revalidates native membership, exact original references/Guid, placement/Blocks, name/shape, named yields and inventory state before returning. A changed or malformed native record fails without publishing a partial packet. This synchronous check is not a lock against later world changes. The provider has no production caller.

- Stockpile and ordinary building each retain one physical Structure body. A stockpile's canonical family identifies its inventory role; it is not a second Storage body. Empty stock retains the catalog building profile. Native `WhitelistedGoodType` is deliberately unused: SmallWarehouse's value is the category `Box`, not a good ID.
- `Inventory.Stock` captures positive actual quantities including reservations. Distinct good catalog profiles are retained separately from quantities, without multiplying or composing fuel. Disabled inventory still retains its stock and reports unavailable effects. Wrong owner or absent role inventory rejects capture instead of pretending it is empty.
- Every named Yielder is retained. Pine has Cuttable/Log and Gatherable/PineResin; Carrot uses Cuttable/RemoveOnCut, and Blueberry uses Gatherable/Berries. Actual native getter amount and declared amount remain separate. Disabled `Yielder.Yield` reports zero despite possibly retained private quantity; `YieldEnabled=false` marks that capture unavailable. No private quantity reflection, fallback refill, or automatic registration is added.
- Water/badwater source entities retain their native footprints separately for future unowned environment initialization. Terrain/soil/water field composition is outside this capture slice. Uninitialized, unpositioned, unfinished, preview, deleted and leftover-tree records are reported explicitly rather than imported as fresh completed bodies.
- Unknown profiles, unsupported families (including infrastructure and GoodStack), ambiguous inventory roles, natural material budget/composition, disabled yields/inventories, vegetation profile mismatch and missing physical reconstruction remain explicit diagnostics. No policy is chosen merely to make a packet usable.

Current scalar facts need separate decisions. Pine has three fuel8 slots and Log2×damage12 body capacity24, plus resin material omitted by the legacy importer. At the inspected base, body damage selected the maximum slot loss per batch, so simultaneous total depletion applied8; a separate body-reducer fix is owned by the coordinator. Carrot is explicitly nonburnable and excluded by the current native crop collector; forcing Organic registration would invent capacity9. Blueberry uses legacy Vegetation fuel10/flammability3 despite its catalog fuel1/flammability1, with Berries3×Organic3 body capacity9. Empty warehouse legacy category fuel1/flammability0 omits its physical fuel2/flammability1. These are preserved input facts and known discrepancies, not normalized by capture.

Validation: native test fixtures use installed Blueprint.zip facts for warehouse/Pine/Carrot/Blueberry; actual installed Blocks/Placement methods prove sparse local footprints, and the production capture helper reads actual managed Yielder getters for partial, zero and disabled states. Model tests cover immutable quantities, overlapping lower bodies, water separation, aliases and unsupported roles. The whole EntityRegistry provider has been compiled but has not been executed on live GameObjects or a loaded world. No engine run, simulation composition, save publication or production initialization is claimed.

Detailed read-only source/API proposal and exact assembly/IL/blueprint evidence: `/tmp/wildfire-initial-world-projection/PROPOSAL.md`. Scalar policy, aggregate family correction/history, and full world admission remain separately owned prerequisites.
