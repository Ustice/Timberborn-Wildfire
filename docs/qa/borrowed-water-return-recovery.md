## Remaining acceptance boundaries

Controller proof must exercise actual PrepareRecovery followed by BindReturn and native initialized equipment/reserver, physical access/arrival, reserved transfer, native full actor save/reload and resumed ordinary work. A finite fixture may supply Water1 and a fixed eligible return inventory; that proves recovery, not river pickup or completed Folktails suppression.

The foreign-current persistence fixture has no outstanding return reservation, matching the final walking design. Full/missing destinations may remain unavailable indefinitely; retained water is conservation, not a promise of eventual delivery.
